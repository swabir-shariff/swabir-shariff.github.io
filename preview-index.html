About

Born and raised in Scarborough, I grew up in Toronto Community Housing. I watched friends — and my own brother — pulled toward the justice system, while the services meant to help them never reached them: no mentors, no one they could see themselves in, outsider-designed solutions. They disengaged.

  • Ages 11–18: one-on-one mentorship
  • youth leadership
  • violence prevention
  • sports
  • co-op placements
  • school-based violence prevention with community partners, funded by the City of Toronto.

What I bring:

Lived experience, built into the design

I was the young person services were built for — every plan starts there.

An operator, not a theorist

I built The Good Guides and run it still — governance, delivery, accountability carried personally.

Executive vantage point

Formed running operations under scrutiny, long before the founder title.

At the policy table

I helped lead a City of Toronto Youth Violence Prevention partnership in Scarborough, and Scarborough's youth engagement through COVID.

As Director of Security, I led round-the-clock (24/7) operations for a major retail centre. Young people skipping school drifted through my corridors; I started hangouts with them. The Good Guides grew out of those conversations — founded in 2020, built youth-led, young people holding the keys. I later stepped away to lead it full time.

In October 2026, that work carried me to London as a QECT 100 Young Leaders Awardee.

A typical engagement

Advisory engagements run 8–12 weeks, in four stages:

1 · Discovery & program assessment

Site visits, interviews — who shows up, who doesn't, why.

2 · Redesign strategy

A written, board-ready plan: staffing, scheduling and program-model changes, in plain language.

3 · Implementation support

Two working sessions a month.

4 · 90-day results review

What moved, what didn't, what's next.

Signature talks

The philosophy

“Keys to the whole house.”

Don't offer young people a seat at the table, or a key to one room — hand them the keys to the whole house: the work theirs to lead, with support behind them. Everyone a productive piece of the whole — nobody a guest, nobody a passenger.

Signature Talk · 01

“Keys to the Whole House”

Real authority for young people over their work — and the trust that follows. A working model for sharing decision-making.

Best for: youth-serving organizations, funders, public institutions.
