About

Born and raised in Scarborough’s Toronto Community Housing, Swabir Shariff watched family and friends pulled toward the justice system while services meant to help never reached them — young people couldn’t see themselves in them and disengaged. He spent years as a Director of Security in Scarborough, leading round-the-clock operations for a major retail centre, where conversations with young people who had stopped showing up to school showed him what was missing. He founded The Good Guides in 2020, while still serving as Director of Security, and later stepped away to lead it full time. Built with young people, not prescribed at them, it has mentored 10,000+ young people across Toronto since 2020, and he has helped direct over a million dollars in investment into The Good Guides and other Scarborough youth initiatives. It delivers school-based violence prevention programming with local community partners, funded by the City of Toronto’s Youth Violence Prevention Grant (2023).

Signature Talks

01 — Keys to the Whole House

Why young people don’t need a seat at someone else’s table — they need the keys: real authority over the work, with real support behind them. A working model for sharing decision-making with the people a program serves, and an honest account of the trust that follows.

02 — Change the Starting Line

The answer to youth violence — and to every system that leaves people behind — is redesigning where people start, not asking more effort from the people the start failed. A framework for finding the starting lines inside your own system, and the first moves to change them.

03 — Every Piece of the Whole

Each of us is a productive piece of a whole none of us can see in full. Leadership as the work of letting each piece land — every person in the room a working part of the result, nobody a guest and nobody a passenger.
