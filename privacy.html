Privacy notice

This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects, which services process it, and how enquiry details are used.

Visits and clicks — GoatCounter

This site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-conscious analytics service, to count page visits and clicks on the main buttons and document downloads — for example, how many people open the media kit. The counts are aggregate: they tell me which parts of the site are used, not who used them. GoatCounter does not set advertising cookies and does not build profiles of visitors for advertisers.

Enquiries — FormSubmit

If you send the enquiry form, the details you enter — your name, organization, email address, enquiry type, optional event date, and your message — are transmitted to FormSubmit, a form-delivery service, which forwards them to my email inbox. I use those details only to reply to your enquiry. Please don't put highly sensitive personal information in the message field.

If JavaScript is disabled in your browser, the same form is delivered by FormSubmit directly and returns you to a thank-you page on this site.

Video and downloads

The showreel video on this site is hosted on this site and plays in your browser; it is not embedded from a video platform, and it loads only when you press play. The biography, media kit and speaker one-sheet PDFs are likewise files on this site. Where this site links out to other websites — LinkedIn, YouTube, news outlets, The Good Guides — those sites are run by their own operators under their own privacy policies.

Hosting — GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub's servers process the technical information needed to deliver pages to your browser, such as your IP address and browser type.

Questions or removal

To ask what I hold from an enquiry you sent, or to have it deleted, email me at swabir.shariff@thegoodguides.org.
